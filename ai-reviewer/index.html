About This Project

A full-stack AI-powered document review application built with FastAPI, SQLite, and DeepSeek R1 via OpenRouter API. Upload PDF or TXT documents and interact with an AI assistant to get insights, summaries, and analysis.

Document Upload

Support for PDF and TXT files with automatic text extraction

AI Chat Interface

Interactive conversation with DeepSeek AI about your documents

Session Management

Automatic cleanup when you close the browser for privacy

Full-Stack Architecture

FastAPI backend, SQLite database, deployed on AWS EC2
